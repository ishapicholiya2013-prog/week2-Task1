About Northstar

Small by choice.
Thoughtful by design.

Northstar is a fictional digital studio created for this project. Its purpose is simple: make everyday digital experiences easier to understand and use.

Our story

The studio concept grew from a familiar problem: many small organisations need a clear online presence, but do not need a complicated website.

Our approach begins with listening, moves through simple prototypes, and ends with a product people can comfortably maintain.

How we work

Ask before building. Understand the real problem.

Keep decisions visible. Explain the tradeoffs.

Test the small details. Forms, navigation and readable layouts matter.
